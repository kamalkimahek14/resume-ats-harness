COMPANY: Larkspur Health Data
TITLE: Senior Data Engineer
NOTE: Invented example post, written for this repository.

Larkspur Health Data helps clinics compare outcomes across sites. Patient records arrive from many systems and
must be cleaned, linked and protected before any analyst can query them.

Responsibilities
You will own our Snowflake warehouse and the dbt project that builds it. You will write data quality tests that
stop a bad feed at the door, document lineage for every table, and work with our privacy officer so that personal
data and HIPAA rules are respected at each step. Most of the tooling is in Python.

Requirements
6+ years of data engineering, at least two of them with dbt and Snowflake.
Deep knowledge of data governance, access control and handling PII.
A bachelor's degree in a quantitative field.

Preferred
Azure experience. Kubernetes for running internal services.
