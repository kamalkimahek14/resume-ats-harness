COMPANY: Copperline Logistics
TITLE: Streaming Data Engineer
NOTE: Invented example post, written for this repository.

Copperline Logistics tracks trucks and parcels across a regional network. Every scan and GPS ping becomes an
event, and dispatchers need those events on their screens within seconds.

In this role you will build real-time pipelines on Apache Kafka and Apache Flink, written in Java or Scala.
You will run them on Kubernetes, keep them inside their SLAs, and join the on-call rotation that answers when a
stream stalls at night.

You should have 5+ years of software or data engineering, with at least two years on streaming systems.

Bonus points
Spark for the nightly reconciliation jobs. Grafana dashboards for the dispatch floor.
