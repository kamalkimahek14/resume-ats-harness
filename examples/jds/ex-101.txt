COMPANY: Northbeam Analytics
TITLE: Data Engineer
NOTE: Invented example post, written for this repository.

Northbeam Analytics sells forecasting software to mid-size retailers. Our data team owns the pipelines that
turn raw point-of-sale files into the tables our forecasting models read every morning.

What you will do
- Design and run batch pipelines in Python and SQL that load customer files into our warehouse on AWS.
- Write Apache Spark jobs for the largest customers, where a single file can hold a billion rows.
- Schedule and monitor work in Apache Airflow, and fix failed runs before the morning model refresh.
- Shape clean data models for the forecasting team.

What you bring
- 4+ years building data pipelines in production.
- Strong Python and SQL.
- Hands-on Spark and Airflow.
- A bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with Kafka or other event streams.
- Terraform for the infrastructure you run.
