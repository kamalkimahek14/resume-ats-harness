TITLE: Data Engineer, Machine Learning
NOTE: Invented example post, written for this repository.

Tallgrass Media publishes podcasts and video. Our recommendation team trains machine learning models on listening
history, and the slowest part of every project is getting features ready.

You will build PySpark jobs on Google Cloud that turn raw listening logs into features, publish them to a feature
store, and check that training and serving read the same values. BigQuery is our warehouse.

We look for 4+ years of data engineering, solid Python, and some exposure to how models are trained.

Nice to have
Statistics background, for example designing A/B tests on recommendations.
